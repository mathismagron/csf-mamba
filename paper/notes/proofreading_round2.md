# Proofreading round 2: main.tex and supplement.tex

Date: 14 Oct 2026. Scope: paper/main.tex and paper/supplement.tex read end to end, generated tables (tables_tex/*.tex) and all 7 figures checked on the rendered PDF pages, bibliography checked for proper nouns lost to IEEEtran's sentence-casing. No result, number or citation was added or removed; claims were changed only to soften them or to remove statements made stale by the controls revision.

After the edits: both PDFs compile (tectonic, env `latex`) with 0 overfull boxes and 0 undefined references or citations; `python paper/scripts/check_numbers.py` reports `178 macros used, 102 distinct literals, 0 problems` (one literal fewer: 2.21 is now the macro \TeacherAdvVitg).

## Errors I consider serious

1. **Sign error, Sec. VI (Teachers).** The text said the fold-0 teacher 'trails by −0.14', a double negative that reads as a 0.14 lead. Fixed (L1).
2. **Stale statement, Sec. V-B.** The text said a decaying learning-rate schedule 'might remove part of that decline ... which we did not test'. The cosine control in Sec. VII / Table VIII tested exactly this (cosine: best 23.52, last 22.79, still declining). Fixed (S1).
3. **Contribution 4 implied that the ground-truth-only control was run on SECOND** ('... removes the gain on these folds and leaves about one third of it on SECOND'). Only self-distillation was run on SECOND. Fixed (I3).
4. **The supplement had an obsolete title** ('DistilSCD: Foundation-Model Accuracy at Lightweight Cost ... with a Location-Disjoint Landsat-SCD Protocol'), different from main.tex. Fixed (SU1).
5. **Supplement S2 predated the revision.** It said the controls were not run on held-out data and that contamination 'cannot explain' the 1.8-point gap. Now: 'unlikely to explain the whole gap', with a pointer to the Landsat-SCD ground-truth-only control (SU5).
6. **The abstract overstated the lower end of the latency range** (4.9×, batch-8 bf16). Table V contains 4.3× (b8 fp16) and 4.2× (b1 bf16), and Limitations already said 4.2–12.5×. The abstract now uses \RatioLatOneBf (4.2×) (A1).

## Open questions for the authors (not edited)

1. **Fig. 6b and the 'six configurations' paragraph (Sec. VIII-A).** The self-distillation arms are new teacher configurations with a teacher advantage of about 0: on SECOND −0.03 (23.87 vs 23.90) with a gain of +0.75, and on Landsat-SCD a gain of −0.04. The first paragraph ('the gain stays between 1.8 and 3.3 SeK while the teacher's advantage ranges ...') covers PerASCD teachers only, and the second paragraph discusses self-distillation separately. Decide whether to say explicitly that the six points are all PerASCD-family teachers, or to add the self-distillation points to Fig. 6b.
2. **Signed macros in running prose.** Phrases such as 'exceed them by +2.61', 'raises SeK by +0.75', 'add +1.52 SeK on top' and 'exceeds the cosine baseline by +2.70' combine a verb of direction with a sign. Removing the sign needs unsigned macro variants in make_numbers.py, so I did not edit them.
3. **Latency range (A1).** I aligned the abstract with Limitations (4.2–12.5×). If you prefer to quote batch size 8 only (4.3–12.5×, since fp16 at b8 is 4.3×), change both places.
4. **Order of first citation of tables.** Table IV (tab:second) is first cited in the Fig. 1 caption, before Table I. IEEE asks for numbering in order of first citation, though captions are usually tolerated. Remove the reference from the caption or accept it.
5. **Fig. 7 placement.** As a [!p] float it lands on page 12, two pages after its first citation (page 10), and after the start of the Discussion. Acceptable for a full-page figure; [t!] on the facing page is an alternative.
6. **Efficiency, Sec. V-D:** 'the counter omits the student's selective scan and the teacher's deformable attention, both small'. No measurement or citation supports 'both small'. Add a supporting figure or drop the two words.
7. **Supplement S3-B:** 'a single-view cache would have given targets inconsistent with the student's augmentation on roughly a tenth of the changed area'. This is derived from 2.2–2.5 % of all pixels against about 20 % changed, but the pixels where the change decision differs are not all changed pixels. Consider 'on about 2.5 % of all pixels'.
8. **Contribution 4 title:** 'Controls that locate the gain in the teacher's targets'. The evidence is three folds × 2–3 seeds plus SECOND self-distillation. 'Controls that tie the gain to the teacher's targets' (the wording of the abstract) would be more cautious.
9. **'e.g.,' / 'i.e.,'.** I used the IEEE comma form (3 places) to remove the inter-sentence space. If you prefer the British form without a comma, write `e.g.\ ` instead.
10. **Thousands separators in generated tables:** Table II row labels ('2385 pairs', '5936 train, 3388 test') and Table SIX ('2968', '1590') have none, whereas the text uses 2\,385. This is generator formatting; I left it because the rows are labels built from CSV strings.
11. **Bibliography title case** is mixed within entries (e.g. [12] 'Knowledge Distillation-Based lightweight ... High-Resolution ... On-Board'; [4] 'Joint Spatio-Temporal modeling'): compounds braced in title case stay capitalised mid-title after IEEEtran sentence-casing. Left to the references agent.
12. **Pre-existing TODOs:** the author list and the corresponding author's e-mail in \thanks.
