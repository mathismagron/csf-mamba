# Target venue and naming (checked 13 Oct 2026)

## IEEE JSTARS — what was verified

- Fully open-access journal for applied remote-sensing and Earth-observation papers;
  English only; submission via ScholarOne (mc.manuscriptcentral.com/jstars).
- Format: IEEE double-column, single-spaced journal template (IEEEtran, `journal` option).
- Article processing charge: **US$1,800** for submissions since 1 Jan 2025 (GRSS JSTARS
  information page); a 2026 IEEE price list from a consortium shows **US$2,160** with a
  subsidised price being phased out. **Confirm the amount at submission.** IEEE student
  members get no discount on APCs. Overlength charges: none found for JSTARS; no strict page
  limit found — aim for 12–14 pages, as is typical for JSTARS regular papers.
- **Open special issue: "Foundation Models and Agents in Earth Observation"**, submissions
  1 Jun – 31 Dec 2026. Distilling a remote-sensing foundation model into a compact student
  fits this scope directly; submitting there is an option to decide before upload.
- Not verified (check on the submission site): graphical abstract requirement, data/code
  availability statement wording, ORCID requirement, cover-letter template.
