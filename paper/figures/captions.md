## Fig. 1 — fig1_sek_vs_gmacs (single column)

Accuracy versus inference cost on the SECOND test set. Knowledge distillation moves the 16.5 M-parameter student (grey) from 23.90 to 26.20 SeK with the PerASCD ViT-G teacher (DistilSCD, dark blue) and to 25.69 with the VMamba-B teacher (light blue), at unchanged cost; the ViT-G teacher (548 M parameters, 1510 GMACs) reaches 26.11. Students: mean of n = 7 (no KD) and n = 5 seeds (KD), error bars = 95 % CI (smaller than the markers for the KD arms); SeK = maximum test SeK over 120 epochs (field convention; final-epoch values in Table II). Teachers: published checkpoints re-evaluated under our protocol, single run. GMACs per 512 × 512 pair. Our models and both teachers are counted with the same counter (PyTorch aten flop counter), which gives the 51× ratio (29.48 vs 1509.74 GMACs; 33.3× fewer parameters); it ignores the teacher's deformable attention and the student's selective scan (fvcore gives 31.42 GMACs for the student), so both counts are lower bounds. Open grey circles: published models on the official split, with accuracies from their papers and complexities as published (`GFLOPs' that are fvcore-style MAC counts, a different counter from aten, hence not strictly comparable); SCanNet as retrained on the official split by the PerASCD authors (complexity from ChangeMamba).

## Fig. 2 — fig2_method (double column)

DistilSCD. Offline, once per dataset, the frozen teacher (PerASCD ViT-G) is run on the 8 views of the dihedral group D4 of every training pair; its logits are stored at the native resolution of its decoder (128 × 128, fp16) with 2C + 1 channels (C class logits per date, class 0 = unchanged, and one change logit; 15 channels on SECOND). During training, the view g produced by the student's flip/rotation augmentation is read from the cache (T1/T2 channels swapped with the temporal swap) and upsampled bilinearly. The student is a siamese VMamba-mini encoder with per-stage concatenation fusion, a change decoder and a shared semantic decoder with a temporal embedding, both with DySample upsampling; the change maps guide the semantic decoder. Loss = task cross-entropies + soft-target BCE on the change logit (λ_chg = 8) + temperature-scaled KL on the semantic distributions renormalised over the real classes (T = 2, λ_sem = 1, all pixels). Only the student is used at inference. Inherited FFT-injection and change-guided-attention details are omitted (no measurable contribution; Supplement).

## Fig. 4 — fig4_screening (double column)

Pre-registered screening on the SECOND internal validation split (2671 training / 297 validation pairs; the test set was not used). 16 arms × 2 seeds = 32 runs of 120 epochs; points = seeds, ticks = means; y = maximum validation SeK over epochs. (a) Weight of the change-KD term (λ_chg = 0: no KD); the two controls at λ = 2 replace the teacher's soft change target by the binary or the smoothed ground truth (same loss, weight and pipeline) and do not improve on no KD. (b) Semantic KL on top of change KD (λ_chg = 8): temperature T and pixel mask (all pixels, or pixels changed according to the ground truth or the teacher); dashed line = change KD only; the retained configuration is T = 2, all pixels. (c) Feature KD added to the retained output KD: encoder stages (fraction of the input resolution) and λ_feat; y = paired-by-seed SeK increment over output KD, x = training time per run (A100-h; 2.3–2.4× that of output KD); peak training memory 4.2 vs 17.4 GB. Two seeds per arm: these are screening results, confirmed on the test set with five seeds (Fig. 3).

## Fig. 5 — fig5_landsat_protocol (double column)

Landsat-SCD: leakage of the random pair split and the location-disjoint protocol. (a) The 12 location indices k of LandsatSCD512 drawn as a 4 × 3 grid (row = first digit, column = second digit; this spatial layout is inferred from the indices, not from georeferencing), coloured by the fold in which each location is a test location (F0–F3, three test locations per fold); "val Fi" marks the single validation location of fold i. Fold 0 is exploratory (Table VII). (b) Test (solid) and validation (dashed) SeK per epoch of the same student without KD on the released random pair split (mean of n = 5 seeds; 69.42 ± 0.17 at epoch 600, still rising) and on the location-disjoint fold 0 (pilot run with a 600-epoch budget, 1 seed, stopped at epoch 477). Dotted line: a lookup oracle that labels each test pair from the training labels of the same two images, without learning (77.87 SeK); in the random split, 477/477 test pairs have both images in the training set. Grey tick: PerASCD as published on the random split (65.21). The random split is the dataset authors' protocol for a single-area time series; the location-disjoint folds measure generalisation to unseen locations instead, and the two sets of numbers are not comparable.

## Fig. 6 — fig6_landsat_results (double column)

Landsat-SCD, location-disjoint folds. (a) Test SeK at the epoch of best fold-validation SeK: PerASCD ViT-G teacher retrained by us with the authors' recipe (one run per fold), student without KD and DistilSCD (3 seeds each; ticks = means). Fold 0 (shaded) failed the pre-registered teacher gate and was run with the gate lifted (exploratory). KD gain on folds 1–3 (primary): +2.53 SeK (95 % CI [0.74, 4.32] across folds; 9/9 seed pairs positive); all four folds: +2.51 (12/12). (b) KD gain versus the teacher's advantage over the no-KD student, for the six teacher/dataset configurations (SECOND: max test SeK, 5 vs 7 seeds; Landsat folds: 3 seed pairs); error bars = ± 1 s.e. of the gain; open marker = fold 0. Points above the dashed y = x line are KD students that outscore their teacher. Pearson r = 0.09 (n = 6, p = 0.87).
